### Section A - General Knowledge (20 marks)
1. How do I log into Redbrick?

### Section B - Redbrick Services (20 marks)
1. What is the purpose of each of the following:
    - `md.redbrick.dcu.ie`
    - `webmail.redbrick.dcu.ie`
    - `wetty.redbrick.dcu.ie`

2. How do I change my password from the command line?
3. How are the weekly workshops organised by Helpdesks called? What is their primary purpose?
4. What are the two servers that you can `ssh` into Redbrick with?
5. True or False: Redbrick provides VMs to members

### Section C - Webspace and Docs (20 marks)
1. What is the URL of your personal Redbrick webpage?
2. I copied my HTML files into my `public_html` folder, but I get a 403 error when visiting my webpage. How do I fix it?
3. What are things I'm not allowed to host on my Redbrick webpage?
4. What is Markdown? Why do people use it? Are you using it right now?
5. Fix the mistakes in the following `~/.ssh/config` file to make yourself able to log into Redbrick:
```

    Host pygmalion.skynet.ie
    UserName Regaus#1337
    IdentityFile /dev/urandom
```

### Section D - Emails (20 marks)

4. How can Redbrick members contact you as a Helpdesk?
5. True or False: `full_name@redbrick.dcu.ie` forwards to `redbrick_username@redbrick.dcu.ie`
